# LeedsPlumber.uk discovery report

Research date: 12 July 2026
Primary source: https://www.leedsplumber.uk/

This report captures the public business, content, proof, design, technical, and conversion information available for the target business before rebranding. Claims marked “verify” should not be carried into final copy without client confirmation.

## 1. Business identity

- Public trading name: LeedsPlumber.uk / Leeds Plumber UK / Leeds Plumber.
- Legal or company name exposed on the website: John McGinty Plumbing Services.
- Owner/operator identity used in reviews: John.
- Primary visible phone number: 0113 418 2075.
- Additional phone number exposed in the LocalBusiness JSON-LD: 07701 047028. Verify which number should be primary.
- Address: 51 Whinmoor Crescent, Leeds, LS14 1NJ.
- Business model: mobile plumbing and drainage service operating from a residential-looking address; there is no showroom or customer-facing office described.
- Core positioning: friendly, practical, reasonably priced, no fuss, fast-response plumbing for Leeds.
- Stated availability: 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
- Stated emergency response: engineer aims to arrive within 1 hour, sometimes sooner.
- Stated charging policy: no call-out charge; charges begin from arrival.
- Other stated offers: free quotes/estimates and an O.A.P. discount.
- Stated trust signals: fully insured engineers, punctuality, courtesy, professionalism.

The public Google Business profile embedded on the site describes the company as a “general repair and emergency plumbing company servicing the entire Leeds area”. It identifies the profile as “Leeds Plumber UK”, with the same address and a Google Maps place ID. The website footer and structured data use John McGinty Plumbing Services, so the rebrand should preserve the legal/trading-name distinction until confirmed.

## 5. Reviews and social proof

### Google review widget on the live site

The live embedded widget reports 5.0 from 36 reviews. The underlying widget data contains 36 reviews, all rated five stars, with the most recent record dated 13 August 2024. This is strong proof, but the count is not necessarily current because the live site’s widget data appears older than the current crawl date.

Recurring themes in the Google reviews:

- Fast response to emergencies, including evenings and weekends.
- Same-day or within-the-hour attendance.
- On-time arrival.
- Friendly, polite, calm, and professional manner.
- Fair or reasonable pricing.
- Problems solved first time.
- Clear explanations and helpful advice.
- Clean, tidy work.
- Willingness to help with small repairs and phone advice.
- Strong repeat-use and recommendation intent.

Useful short proof language from the widget includes “Very responsive professional plumber”, “Quick toilet flush repair”, “Arrived approx. 15 minutes after I called”, “Quick to arrive, good quality work”, and “Excellent service would highly recommend”. Use these only after confirming permission and current review-display requirements.

### FreeIndex profile

The FreeIndex profile reports 79 reviews, membership of 17+ years, and a rating distribution of 65 five-star, 8 four-star, 1 three-star, 3 two-star, and 2 one-star reviews. It lists the same address and website, plus a partially masked mobile number. The profile was marked updated on 22 May 2026 and lists opening hours as 07:00–19:00, which conflicts with the website’s 24/7 claim. The profile also describes free quotes, no call-out charge, and a one-hour response promise.

FreeIndex contains detailed positive reviews praising long-term reliability, emergency attendance, fair pricing, clear communication, branded parts, and competent repairs. It also contains negative historical reviews alleging a call-out/pricing dispute and property damage/aggressive conduct. These are old, but they are public and should be treated as a reputation-management issue rather than ignored.

### Other public listings

- Cylex lists the business as Leeds Plumber uk, with the same address and phone, 24-hour opening hours, and a broad list of plumbing, drainage, pump, shower, pipe, tank, and installation services.
- Abbey Plumbers describes the business as a repair-focused, rapid-response Leeds service and reports a conflicting 4.3 Google rating from 52 reviews. Treat this as third-party aggregation that needs verification.
- FreeIndex reports 79 reviews and a lower mixed rating distribution than the 5.0/36 Google widget.
- These discrepancies mean the new website should avoid combining review counts from different platforms into one headline.

## 6. Visual and design system

### Layout

- Built with Wix Website Builder / Wix Thunderbolt runtime.
- Fixed-width canvas measured at approximately 980px.
- Desktop page geometry on the home route: header about 370px, main content about 705px, footer about 132px.
- The header is sticky with a high z-index.
- Mobile is not genuinely responsive: at a 390px viewport, the site still renders a 980px-wide header and main area and produces a document width of approximately 1,056px. Users must horizontally scroll or view a cropped desktop layout.
- The visual composition is a single pale-blue canvas with a large header treatment, compact nav strip, sparse welcome page, and black footer.

- Pale blue canvas: `#E7EFF7` / `rgb(231, 239, 247)`.
- Primary red: `#DD0000` / `rgb(221, 0, 0)`.
- Deep navy: `#2C3258` / `rgb(44, 50, 88)`.

- External reviews widget introduces additional orange, yellow, blue, and dark-navy tones; these are not part of the core site system.

The central visual move is a pale-blue background with a large red rectangular call panel, black footer, and navy/indigo iconography. The core identity is more “bright local utility service” than luxury trade brand.

## 8. Rebrand implications

### Preserve or translate

- Preserve the phone-first conversion model, but make the number a real click-to-call link.
- Preserve “no call-out charge” and “charges from arrival” only if still commercially accurate.
- Preserve the clear emergency response proposition, but separate “available 24/7” from “aims to arrive within one hour” so the claim is precise.
- Translate the strong proof themes into a modern proof system: fast response, fair pricing, clear explanations, tidy work, repeat customers, and local coverage.
- Use the current service inventory as discovery input, not as final confirmed scope.
- Replace the current fixed-width Wix composition with the existing project’s responsive React system.

### Verify before final copy

- Correct business/trading/legal name.
- Primary phone number and whether the 07701 mobile should be displayed.
- 24/7 availability and one-hour response coverage.
- No call-out charge and “from arrival” pricing wording.
- O.A.P. discount.
- Fully insured / fully qualified / CHAS / NVQ claims.
- Whether bathroom fitting, boiler work, gas work, or heating work is offered.
- Current service area beyond Leeds.
- Correct current Google review count and rating.
- Permission to reuse named review excerpts.
- Current social accounts and whether LinkedIn is official.
- Whether the Whinmoor Crescent address should be displayed publicly.

### 10K checklist read-through

- Point of view: the source has a clear bright-blue/red emergency-utility direction, but the rebrand should commit to a more deliberate trade positioning.
- Typography: source hierarchy exists, but the mixed Wix font declarations should be replaced with one intentional display/body pairing.
- Colour: the core palette is restrained enough, though the review widget adds unrelated colours.
- Hierarchy: the source relies on oversized header text and sparse page composition; the rebrand can retain clarity while improving content depth.
- Imagery: the source has almost no meaningful project photography, so commissioned or tightly curated plumbing imagery will be important.
- Motion: the source is mostly static, which is useful; any new motion should remain restrained.
- Mobile: this is the largest source failure; the fixed canvas must not be carried forward.
- Invisible expensive details: the rebrand should add true responsive behaviour, click-to-call, keyboard-safe navigation, persistent form labels, precise metadata, structured local business data, and accessible proof components.
